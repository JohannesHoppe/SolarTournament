# docs

Design documents for parts of the game that are planned or being built, written for the people who build them. The game's rules as they are live in the repository's `README.md`; what is built from a document here moves into it, with its numbers, in the same commit.

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `campaign/` | The solo campaign: its story, worlds, characters, missions and progression |
| `worlds/` | Flying over worlds: races and fights low over a world's ground under its gravity, the hover field, lift in air, tracks from one place to another, the overdrive's one rule |
