# public/assets/people

The campaign's people, written by `tools/people` (see its README): their busts as their holograms call in (`rask.bin`, `pim.bin`, `hedda.bin`, `saw.bin` for the Saw Brothers, `tally.bin` for Ma Tally, `navy.bin` for the Navy cutter's officer, `jack.bin` for Hollow Jack, `crisp.bin` for Bellamy Crisp, `spare.bin` for Spare, `quill.bin` for Commissioner Quill, `vey.bin` for Admiral Vey, `perihelion.bin` for Perihelion, `kesh.bin` for Kesh, `president.bin` for the President, about 3,600 triangles each, in the format `bustParts` in `src/view/products.ts` reads) and their figures in the flesh for the scenes in person (`rask.glb`, `pim.glb`, `hedda.glb`, `quill.glb`, `spare.glb`, `president.glb`, `crisp.glb`, `kesh.glb`: binary glTF, posed, 12,000 to 19,000 faces, textured, under 1.4 MB each); and the Derelict's frozen dead (`frozen-0.glb` to `frozen-13.glb`, written by `tools/people/frozen.py`: eight colonists of the world ship posed as they froze (their skin as the vacuum left it, grey-white, blue-grey at the lips and fingertips), and six of its crew in the ship's light emergency suits, three of the astronaut and three of the SWAT figure of Quaternius's Ultimate Modular Men Pack (CC0 1.0, https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html; its flat colors laid into small worn textures, posed and thinned out, near about 5,600 triangles and far 960), each a mesh `near` of about 3,500 triangles and `far` of about 600, a pair half again as many, their textures in an opaque JPEG atlas and a cut-out PNG one for the hair, 200 to 500 KB each, 2.3 MB together).

They are made from figures of [MakeHuman](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/) through its Blender extension MPFB: the base mesh, its targets, the clothes, hair and eyebrows of MakeHuman's system assets are CC0, and MakeHuman makes no claim on what is made with them ([`LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md), sections C and D). Shaped, cut out and thinned for the game.

`suit-astronaut.glb` and `suit-swat.glb`: those two figures as `tools/people/frozen.py` writes them, rigged, in its rest pose, without its animations, its colors as small worn textures: the suit the PAAA 01's crew died in and the clones fly in (README, "The universe"), for whatever shows one, and the script's own input in place of the pack's glTFs.
