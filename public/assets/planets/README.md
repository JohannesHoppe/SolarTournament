# public/assets/planets

The maps of the planets drawn from real images (see `src/world/landmarks.ts` and `src/world/junkyard.ts`, loaded by `src/assets.ts`). Saturn's and Jupiter's maps are cylindrical: longitude across, latitude from the north pole at the top to the south pole at the bottom. Earth's show it as seen from far above the point beneath its arena (35° north, 20° east): an orthographic projection about that point, north up, east to the right, the square reaching 62° from it to every side (its sine, 0.883 Earth radii, from the middle to an edge), black outside the planet's circle; the night map is the whole square, the day map its western 62 %, the part the sun or the twilight reaches (see `MAP_REACH` and `DAY_SHARE` in `src/world/junkyard.ts`). The page loads the `-1k` maps; the `-2k` ones only once the picture's quality asks for them (see `Landmarks.setDetail`).
