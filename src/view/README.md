# src/view

What the screen shows besides the 3D world: the HUD as DOM elements, and the passes and effects drawn over the picture. Nothing here changes the game's state.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `hud.ts` | The flight computer's projection: gauges, markers (the next ring's brackets and where the flight crosses its plane among them, the crosshair orange over a hull, the target's range and closure rate; the ships' markers, their arrows at the screen's edge with the ships' names placed clear of each other and of the HUD), the aids of free drift (the dashed line from the crosshair to the Course marker, the Course arrow at the screen's edge with its name placed like a ship's, the Course marker's flash on switching), the fixed references (center, sun, the arena's big things, bases, battleship) at their place or the screen's edge, height and climb, the alarms and the escape gauge, the round's panel, the boot of a fresh clone's link; writes the DOM only when a value changes |
| `screen.ts` | Where the HUD's marks land on screen, as plain geometry: a point held on the frame off screen, the edge an arrow sits at, a free box for a name beside it, the line between two marks, and when the aids of free drift show (tested in `screen.test.ts`) |
| `streaks.ts` | The motion streaks: faint azure dashes flowing over a canvas of their own out of where the ship moves (across the screen while it slides sideways, into the Brake marker while it flies backwards), always on while it moves; standing still with reduced motion (tested in `streaks.test.ts`) |
| `overlay.ts` | A canvas over the whole picture the HUD draws on in CSS pixels, sized with the picture's resolution: what the attitude and the motion streaks share |
| `attitude.ts` | Horizon line, pitch ladder, bank scale with its sky pointer and heading tape on a canvas, as in a fighter's head-up display; as sharp as the picture's resolution, drawn anew only when the camera turns |
| `plane.ts` | The reference plane's polar grid and the drop lines from ships, gates and the waypoint down or up to it: lines over everything, faded with distance, built once |
| `radar.ts` | The radar sphere on a canvas |
| `effects.ts` | Particles and laser beams, pooled, fading in a planet's haze; a cheaper picture emits a share of the particles; the maneuvering thrusters' puffs, laid anew each frame at the firing nozzles, fewer points on a cheaper picture |
| `killcam.ts` | Records the last seconds and replays the own clone's death in slow motion, each ship put between the two kept frames around the moment shown (tested in `killcam.test.ts`) |
| `chase.ts` | The chase camera's field of view, how fast it follows and where it hangs, looking along the nose |
| `guide.ts` | The racing line: a dashed ribbon with pulses of light, orange in the brake zones |
| `ghost.ts` | The best lap's hologram |
| `impostor.ts` | A billboard for a shape seen from far away (the belt's rocks, the field's farthest rocks and ice): a camera-facing quad showing one of a few pre-rendered views, lit from the shape's own baked normal map and the real sun so it tracks a solar flare, in a planet's haze as everything is; an ice piece's painted in the ice's color and glow (tested in `impostor.test.ts`) |
| `showcase.ts` | The title screen: a landing pad of gunmetal and steel with the seal inlaid in gold, the ship hovering above it, turning slowly on the spot and throwing its shadow onto it |
| `glitch.ts` | The disturbed link: a corrupted-stream shader and the HUD's glitch |
| `datamosh.ts` | The clone's death: its last picture rots |
| `lensing.ts` | The black hole's gravitational lens; its round black shadow, with the disk's near side crossing in front of it (the disk's gas summed along each pixel's ray, see world/landmarks), and the thin photon ring; a cheaper picture marches fewer steps along each pixel's ray looking for its source |
| `reentry.ts` | A ship flying hot through a planet's air, from its own altitude and speed (the same for every ship): the shock layer of plasma on the side it flies towards with its tail, the plasma trail, the hull's glow, sparks and flakes once it burns, the fireball of a ship that dies burning and its pieces sinking, the plasma licking past the edges of the view; two draw calls per hot ship, the rest particles of `effects.ts` (tested in `reentry.test.ts`) |
| `haze.ts` | A planet's haze in the picture: three.js's fog chunks as a height fog summing the haze along each view ray in closed form, as world/air does, in every material's shader while the scene carries a fog (only in an arena with air), glows only fading; under the deck the clouds in its light (banks, belts and towers from a noise texture, carried by the wind, shoved about in swirls, brighter looking up), which change its color, never its sum (the noise read back as the GPU reads it in `haze.test.ts`); lightning lighting it; how much of the sun's and the sky's light reaches the camera |
| `textures.ts` | Canvas textures: glows, sparks, smoke, the thrusters' cold gas, and the canvas every texture drawn in code starts from; the running light of the neon trim |
| `surfaces.ts` | The surfaces of everything built in code: worn depot paint (and where its lacquer is gone), the world ship's craters and burns, the battleship's polished armor plates, the rings' plating; texture coordinates for flat faces, the reflection map |
| `lettering.ts` | Paint on a hull: a name and registry in stenciled block letters built from bars (the same on every device), the emblem of the united Earth, a livery stripe, weathered (faded, chipped, scratched, pitted), drawn on a canvas with transparency for a decal |
| `marks.ts` | The bot or Host mark before a name, the swatch of a pilot's tint beside it, and the star that marks the lead browser in the standings |
| `ink.ts` | What an element shows on screen (its plate, or the text and children inside it; a fixed reference's glyph and label): the HUD keeps the names at the edge clear of it, the layout test measures by it |
| `quality.ts` | The picture's quality: the ladder of looks from the full picture to the cheapest (resolution, bloom, shadows, lacquer, particles, rock detail, the belt, Jupiter's cap and sheets of cloud, the black hole's lens, the title screen's pad), the tiers low, medium and high as presets on it, the guess of a device's tier, the Host's choice as the browser keeps it, the governor that moves along the ladder with the frame rate (telling a screen capped below the target frame rate from a slow device; one each for the menu and a running round), the benchmark's features (tested in `quality.test.ts`) |
| `graphics.ts` | Applies the rung of the ladder to the renderer, the bloom, the sun's shadows, the hulls' lacquer, the particles, the ring's grains, the rocks, the belt, the Jupiter landmark, the black hole's lens, the title screen's pad and the reference plane's grid; feeds the governor the frame times and holds back a knob that compiles shaders until a hitch goes unseen |
| `bloom.ts` | The bloom at a share of the picture's resolution |
| `grade.ts` | The output pass (tone mapping, the screen's encoding) with the arena's color grade in the same shader: a tint, a color in the shadows, the saturation |

The styles of all of it live in `src/style.css`.
