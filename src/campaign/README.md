# src/campaign

Lot 7731, the solo campaign (its design in `docs/campaign/`). It is built mission by mission; while it is in the making the title screen shows it without a click, and the address with `?campaign` lets it be flown.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `campaign.ts` | The campaign's state as the browser keeps it (the missions done, the drill reached in the running one, the wallet, the debt still owed, the ratings and their ranks (`RANKS`, `rankOf`), the parts fitted, Nicole's modules installed), the ship as far as it is fitted (`hullOf`: the scrap hull, below any of the Games', with the mining cutter once fitted), the modules of the flight computer (`Module`: the HUD's pieces a salvaged Nicole lacks until a chapter brings them), what the job board calls the parts and modules (`PART_NAMES`, `MODULE_NAMES`), and a mission done: what it fits, its pay less the yard's cut while the debt is unpaid |
| `missions.ts` | The missions in story order, each a run of drills on the flight school's machinery (`FlightSchool` with a mission instead of a course) at a place of the campaign: the prologue and chapter 1 in the Boneyard (Cold Boot: Nicole booting and crashing, Rask's call, out of the rack, down the lane and onto bay 3's pad; then Lamplighter, Live Target, Canister Sweep, The Cutter, First Cut and Docking Lane, each briefed by Rask and begun on the pad, one running on into the next); chapter 2 at Rheasilvia on Vesta: the arrival, where Pim fits Nicole a voice board, Claim Survey, the claims' buoys in the order they were filed, Cut and Haul, the ore of the claim beside a working rig, a beam on the rig fined, Saw Brothers, three claim-jumpers sawing at a rig, two to shoot down before it falls, and The Peak Run, two laps through six old pipes against three rivals, its prize the thrusters rebuilt to stock |
| `scenes.ts` | The scenes in person: for the moments that count the clone climbs out of the cockpit and stands among the people in the flesh (`planOf`: who stands where, the clone's eyes and first look; `stageOf`: the stage around the scene's set); the first, Rask's office in the cab on the tower, where Rask hands the Host on to Hedda Kroll |
| `campaign.test.ts` | The pay and the cut, the cutter fitted on the debt, the scrap hull, Nicole without her modules, the ratings counted once and the rank they raise, the state read back and refused, every mission with a name and drills |
| `scenes.test.ts` | Every scene's plan: the people inside its set, a few meters from the clone and facing it, the clone looking at the first of them |
| `missions.test.ts` | The Peak Run is flown by a middling bot: both laps through every ring round Rheasilvia's claims, nothing missed, without a crash |
