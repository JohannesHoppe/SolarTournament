# src/campaign

Lot 7731, the solo campaign (its design in `docs/campaign/`). It is built mission by mission; while it is in the making the title screen shows it without a click, and the address with `?campaign` lets it be flown.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `campaign.ts` | The campaign's state as the browser keeps it (the missions done, the drill reached in the running one, the wallet, the debt still owed, the ratings and their ranks (`RANKS`, `rankOf`), the parts fitted, Nicole's modules installed, whether the benefactor holds the debt, the choices the story asked of the Host (`Choice`)), the ship as far as it is fitted (`hullOf`: the scrap hull, below any of the Games', with the mining cutter once fitted, the military laser in its place, the old livery once the grey is ground off, the left flank bare until its armor panels are fitted, the radiators a step up with Radiators I, the plating with Plating I), the modules of the flight computer (`Module`: the HUD's pieces a salvaged Nicole lacks until a chapter brings them), what the job board calls the parts and modules (`PART_NAMES`, `MODULE_NAMES`), a mission done: what it fits, its pay less the yard's cut while the debt is unpaid, its ratings by the choices made (`ratingsOf`), the military laser's, the drive's overhaul's, the armor panels', the radiators' and the plating's prices (`LASER_PRICE`, `OVERHAUL_PRICE`, `PANELS_PRICE`, `RADIATORS_PRICE`, `PLATING_PRICE`), and each place's sound (`PLACE_MUSIC`: the flavors of its calm and its fights) |
| `missions.ts` | The missions in story order, each a run of drills on the flight school's machinery (`FlightSchool` with a mission instead of a course) at a place of the campaign: the prologue and chapter 1 in the Boneyard (Cold Boot: Nicole booting and crashing, Rask's call, out of the rack, down the lane and onto bay 3's pad; then Lamplighter, Live Target, Canister Sweep, The Cutter, First Cut and Docking Lane, each briefed by Rask and begun on the pad, one running on into the next); chapter 2 at Rheasilvia on Vesta: the launch ring's choice of the gel or a print on the debt, the galley, the arrival, where Pim fits Nicole a voice board, Claim Survey, the claims' buoys in the order they were filed, Cut and Haul, the ore of the claim beside a working rig, a beam on the rig fined, Saw Brothers, three claim-jumpers sawing at a rig, two to shoot down before it falls, and The Peak Run, two laps through six old pipes against three rivals, its prize the thrusters rebuilt to stock, and Practice Log, the first echo before a lit tunnel; chapter 3 at Occator on Ceres: the arrival, the clone held before the mouth of the market's gallery, and Surplus, the military laser paid from the wallet and the rest put on the debt, Nicole's targeting with it, and Rask's word that he sold the debt to the Tallymen, and Ore Barge, a barge flown out of the crater on its lane through two waves of scrappers firing at its hull, and Repo Men, the Tallymen's tug reeling the hull in on its harpoon's cable until its winch is shot or three of its guards are down, and Wall Run at the Solar Wall, Hedda's barge of refugees following the hull's way over the wall's top edge under its sweeping searchlights, seen twice failing it, and Scout Two, the depot's grey ground off and the scout's old livery under it, the second echo before the wall's second step, and Overhaul, the drive's cracked bell replaced, the main drive to stock; chapter 4 at Stickney on Phobos: the Stickney Grand, Nicole's racing line installed, three laps of eight rings down into the crater against Perihelion and four others, home among the first three; Debt Day, Ma Tally's six collectors over the grooves, four shot down, the Navy's hail, and the benefactor holding the debt from then on (`Campaign.held`: no cut); Groove Shoot, 30 s low in a groove beyond the rim; Armor Panels, the bare left flank closed; chapter 5 at Valhalla on Callisto: Pilot Episode, Nicole's radar installed, the Tallyman Red Ledger and two wingmen over the craters, Red Ledger shot down ending it (`leaderDown`); Ice Run, three ice tugs across the craters, the Loose harpooning them off their lane (`convoyAt`), at most one lost; Valhalla Sprint, six coils in a straight line over the ridges against a clock started at the first; Callisto Echo, the third echo over the Navy's old listening post; Radiators I, the radiators a step up; chapter 6 at the Lineae on Europa: Dose, an ice tanker out of the Crack and through the radiation belt, the clone's dose taken the higher it flies (`doseRate`, `tankerAt`), the Loose harpooning the tanker, Nicole's threat warnings installed; Under the Lineae, down the Crack to the science station on its floor, the fourth echo and Nicole's own name; The Quiet Man, a raider who never finishes a clone and turns away, shot down or let go (the choice kept in `Campaign.choices`, the ratings by it); Plating I, every hit hurting a step less; chapter 7 at Flux on Io: the Flux Tube Race, two laps round eight rings past the flux tube against Perihelion and four miners, the link crackling near the tube. Every echo at a fixed place is one drill (`echoAt`). One race drill (`race`) flies the Peak Run's pipes and the Grand's rings alike |
| `scenes.ts` | The scenes in person: for the moments that count the clone climbs out of the cockpit and stands among the people in the flesh (`planOf`: who stands where, the clone's eyes and first look; `stageOf`: the stage around the scene's set); the first, Rask's office in the cab on the tower, where Rask hands the Host on to Hedda Kroll; the second, Patience's galley over Vesta, Hedda and Pim at table and counter |
| `campaign.test.ts` | The pay and the cut, the cutter fitted on the debt, the scrap hull, Nicole without her modules, the ratings counted once and the rank they raise, the state read back and refused, every mission with a name and drills |
| `scenes.test.ts` | Every scene's plan (the office, the galley): the people inside its set, a few meters from the clone and facing it, the clone looking at the first of them |
| `missions.test.ts` | The campaign's races are flown by a middling bot, nothing missed, without a crash: the Peak Run's two laps round Rheasilvia's claims, the Stickney Grand's three laps into the crater, and the Valhalla Sprint's six coils within its clock, and the Flux Tube Race's two laps past the flux tube; the Saw Brothers' music turns to the fight; the Ore Barge's lane runs clear of Occator, the Ice Run's convoy clear of Valhalla's ground and the listening post, the Dose tanker's lane clear of Europa's ice and the dose taken by height; the second, third and fourth echoes lie clear of their places, inside the arena, the third over the listening post standing solid |
