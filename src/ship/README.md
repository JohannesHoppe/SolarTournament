# src/ship

The ships and who flies them. Human and alien interceptor (and the Sovereign) fly by the same rules; only their looks differ.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `ship.ts` | A ship: its body (an interceptor's mass, see world/bodies), hull, hit zones, holes and decompression, laser heat (and how much its radiators shed where it is, and how they glow with it; another browser's ship warmed by the shots seen), the clone's g-force strain, drive animation, the puffs of its thrusters' nozzles (another browser's ship read from how its velocity and spin change); how one ship's nose lies on another, as the lock warning and the bots read it; the laser's reach and the first hull on a line, where a beam ends whichever side the ship is on; its state over the network, and how a new lead adopts a bot |
| `ship.test.ts` | A bot adopted by a new lead flies on with the torpedoes its last state told of, up to a fresh load; the count goes out with the state whenever it changes |
| `flight.ts` | The Newtonian flight model and its flight computer (`steer()`), and the autopilot (`flyByNose`): the ship flies where its nose points, drive and thrusters taking out the drift across the nose, the nose leading the flight no farther than they bring it back within a second (`leadLimit`); the hardest turn at a speed (`turnRadius`, `cornerSpeed`) |
| `flight.test.ts` | The autopilot with realistic input (`src/test/cockpit.ts`): the nose at rest, the flight back on the nose after a turn at every speed and within the lead's limit, a hand turning the nose to a goal, a turn about at 150 m/s with the brake, the autopilot taking over from free drift, switching modes in a turn, gravity, the hardest turn and race bends at full speed; free drift keeping the flight; a recorded flight replaying the same |
| `computer.ts` | The own ship's flight computer besides the autopilot: the upright hold, the brake to rest, the aim assist; the game and the simulated Host of the tests fly with it |
| `impact.ts` | Striking a surface: the bounce and the scrape (a bump of bodies, see world/bodies; a livelier body passes its own restitution), the damage; two ships ramming each other, hurt by where they are struck |
| `models.ts` | The interceptors' low-poly hulls, built in code, each in coats of its own wear (see `hullSurface` in `view/surfaces.ts`: the human interceptor's paint and its ribbed fins, the alien skin of scales under a film that plays in colors with its faintly glowing rims and outline, the Sovereign's flush ivory plates without a rivet and its engraved gold; the Sovereign's hull number painted on as a decal at one place on both flanks of its nose, see `numberTexture` in `view/lettering.ts`) under a clear lacquer, shaded round with hard edges, their relief a normal map; the hull's shader shows bare steel where the paint chipped off and takes each part's finish (soot is dull) from its color; open drive bells, heat-tinted and sooty inside, with cooling rings; the light strips of the neon trim fitted along their flanks and across their sterns, hidden until worn, and the glowing skin over their radiators (ribs and vents, side blades, delta wings), hidden while cool; the four clusters of maneuvering thrusters where each hull carries them (`THRUSTER_LAYOUTS`), beveled housings with open bells |
| `thrusters.ts` | The maneuvering thrusters as built: four clusters of four nozzles at the hull's corners; which nozzles fire for a push (one side together, fore and aft balanced) and which couples for a turn (opposite corners, opposite ways), for the puffs on screen and the handbook |
| `thrusters.test.ts` | Every hull's thrusters push it every way without turning it and turn it about every axis either way without moving it; a slide left puffs from the right flank |
| `bots.ts` | The bot AI: where to fly, dogfights, aiming with spread (wider under g-force strain), keeping clear of rock along the tube its hull sweeps, of other ships and of the rings' housings, shoving a rival into rock, a hand that eases commands in, the timing of a race's start, the skills of each bot; it knows only what a Host's sensors show, in a planet's air the haze, the safe speed and the heat alarm too, and keeps under the one and climbs out on the other; near a massive body it burns away once its escape gauge reads 30 %; friendly fire: no shot or torpedo while a ship of its own side is in the line of fire (`allyInLine`), a nova core never with one of its own side near, among rivals, at the battleship's parts, on less the longer it holds it (`novaWorth`) |
| `bots.test.ts` | Bots fly by the Hosts' rules: their commands stay within a Host's stick, drive and thrusters and never move the ship themselves, no target behind rock, beyond sight or in a storm, a thief chased but not fired at unseen, the hull kept clear of a rock the line of flight only just misses, hunters read from noses and only within the bot's own sight, the even skills of a made-up name (also one every object inherits), the start's timing, the skill curve (the better, the quicker and closer, but never faster than a quick Host nor exact); in Jupiter's air under the safe speed without ever burning, climbing out on the heat alarm, a ship found by radar beyond the eye but not fired at, one lost that dived under the deck; near the black hole never falling in, neither for a probe on its far side nor out of a dive at the limit; the clone's strain and the nose on a ship as the sensors see it |
| `tints.ts` | The colors ships are marked in: the palette of neon tints a Host picks from (kept in the browser), each bot's own, the Sovereign's ivory and gold, how two Hosts with the same choice are told apart, the side's color in Capture the Flag |
| `tints.test.ts` | The palette: bright, clear of the HUD's azure, the sides' colors, red and each other; every bot its own tint; the tie rule between Hosts |

Bots fly by the same rules as Hosts and know only what a Host's sensors show; a new ability of a ship is given to bots through their controls, never by setting their state. Bots are tuned with the balance runs (`npm run balance`, see `e2e/`): run them after every change here.
