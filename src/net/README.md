# src/net

The online tournament, without a game server: browsers find each other through public Nostr relays and then talk directly over WebRTC (Trystero). One browser leads (the one longest in the room) and runs the bots and the shared rules; every browser runs its own ship and decides its own shots.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `transport.ts` | The wire: Trystero (over the relays of `relays.json`, Trystero's own when none of them answers within 10 seconds; with STUN and the TURN relay from `/turn`), or a BroadcastChannel for local tests (`?local=1`); the protocol's version in the app id, so builds that would misread each other never meet |
| `push.ts` | The notification when a public tournament opens, in the browser: whether it can be had (the browser, and `/push` naming its key), subscribing and unsubscribing through the service worker `public/push-sw.js`, the lead browser's word that its public tournament is open (`OpenBeat`, every two minutes) |
| `pushtext.ts` | The notification's text and the page it opens, in the Host's language |
| `admin.test.ts` | The admin page (`functions/admin.ts`): only a valid Access token of its team and application is let in, not found without, the figures shown |
| `push.test.ts` | The rules of `functions/push.ts`: a subscription checked, kept in its language and with its number, forgotten, each Host told only once its number of open tournaments is reached, a new tournament notified and one still open never again, at most one notification every 15 minutes, no room code kept, the daily budget of writes, the cap on subscriptions, a subscription the push service says is gone forgotten |
| `webpush.ts` | Web Push from the server side on WebCrypto alone: a message encrypted for one browser's subscription (RFC 8291, aes128gcm), the VAPID token naming the sender (RFC 8292, ES256), a push as a fetch's arguments; for the Pages Functions, never run in the page |
| `webpush.test.ts` | Encrypts RFC 8291's example to its very bytes, a fresh key and salt for every message, a VAPID token its public key verifies, the push's headers |
| `turn.test.ts` | The relay's credentials function (`functions/turn.ts`) names only a fixed reason when it hands out nothing, never what an upstream service answered |
| `relays.json` | The public Nostr relays every browser connects to; written by `npm run relays` (`tools/relays/`), checked every week, never edited by hand |
| `session.ts` | The protocol: message types (a hook's hold: rock, ship, core, torpedo, whose browser then decides for it, or probe, reeled in on every browser; a ram of the battleship, from the lead that flies it only; a contact of the field's pieces, from the lead only; a slice of the lead's field `fs`, see world/fieldsync, from the lead only; a ship's self-destruct `sd`, where it went off), rate limits, who leads, starting over after a lost link, the ping to every peer (once a second, the median of its last few round trips); the peers each browser heard come before it, vouching for a peer reached late; starting over to take the field again from a lead it missed it from |
| `online.ts` | Who owns which ship, what goes over the network, the lead's bots; the peer a ship's ping is measured to (a bot's is the lead that runs it) and whether a Host's ship belongs to the browser that leads |
| `validate.ts` | Checks the shape of every message from other browsers (a slice of the lead's field: of its turn, each family's pieces its own) |
| `plausible.ts` | Checks whether what a browser reports could have happened (a motion in the lead's sync of the field no faster than a ram's, jumps, the nose's turn and the beams along it, novas and their hits on the battleship, a self-destruct only where its ship was, torpedoes, a torpedo or probe claimed by a hook only within its reach, kills, each probe taken once, the evidence of hits; score, gates and a race's finish taken no faster than a ship makes them; a ram of the lead's battleship only for a piece at its hull; a contact of the field's pieces only for a piece near something it could have struck, pushed no farther than 50 m and slower than 250 m/s; either blow dated within 3 s of the own field clock; a torpedo's blast and a nova core counted once on each part of the battleship), on the time its states were sent; a ship that fails a check again and again gets its Host reported, and a majority of the flying Hosts casts out a cheater (the Host named never casts itself out); what it keeps of a browser's ships goes when it leaves |
| `room.ts` | A room as its link describes it: code, name, mode, bots; the link a friend follows into it (shared by the share button) |
| `lobby.ts` | Public tournaments announce themselves; the list listens |

Every message from outside is untrusted: it passes `validate.ts`, then the plausibility checks. Test with two tabs (`?local=1#t=<code>`) or `npm run e2e`; `datenschutz.html` names every data flow that goes over the network.
