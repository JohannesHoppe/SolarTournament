# src/game

The round and its rules. `game.ts` holds the `Game` class: states (menu, playing, paused, results), the round's flow, lasers, collisions, the camera and the clone's body; it hands the other parts of this folder what they need each frame.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `game.ts` | The game: round flow and each round's opening show, scoring, respawns, warnings, the menus' wiring; what is solid besides the field (rings, wreckage, cores, probes, torpedoes, a comet's head) for torpedoes, wreckage, probes and the laser; the field's contacts run by the lead |
| `alarms.ts` | The tiered alarms (caution, warning, critical), their tones (a countdown beat for an impact or a torpedo), the escape load of a gravity well, and its grip, which nothing the game places (a new clone, a gate, a base, a probe) lies in |
| `orbit.ts` | The flight computer's orbit readout near the black hole: the conic a ship would fly in free drift (its periapsis and apoapsis), whether it is a stable orbit (bound, its periapsis out of the CRITICAL grip, its apoapsis inside the arena), ORBIT READY or STABLE ORBIT or what is missing, where an orbit fits at all (not over Jupiter), the well's alarm quiet while one is flown, and when Nicole says so; `orbit.test.ts` checks it against the flight model |
| `race.ts` | Round and room modes; when a round takes a new start from its clock (online only by a jitter's worth once known); the race course of gates (a smooth loop of the shape that runs through least rock, diving through a ring's layer, its gates off the racing line's bows, flown at the speed limit; in a planet's air along its cloud deck), its points, where a flight crosses a ring's plane, and how a racer comes at its next gate clear of the housings |
| `survival.ts` | Last Clone Standing: clones per round, the solar flare's plasma coming from the sun and the safe pocket it leaves |
| `flags.ts` | Capture the Flag: sides, bases, sponsor cores, taking one (touch or hook shot), captures (the core itself through the ring); a core in tow as a trailer on the hook's rope (mass, jerks, bumps), adrift with its thrusters braking, also when its tow cable snaps on rock, bouncing off the other core, lost in a well or to a comet's head; the way a carrier flies home through the ring; how a core is shown (faded where it would hide the ship or the crosshair) |
| `fleet.ts` | The President's Fleet: the battleship (laid out in design units and grown by `FLEET_SCALE` into the world), its hangar tunnels to fly through (in world units), its parts, phases and sinking, the hits a torpedo and a nova core deal its parts; how the lead browser flies it on its lane, out of a comet's way and clear of the field's heavy pieces, ramming the light ones aside by their masses; what collides with it (hull, turrets, bells, parked fighters, the bared reactor) and takes hits as its parts; `fleet.test.ts` flies it through a whole round of every arena with comets, wreckage and probes thrown at it and finds nothing in its hull and no heavy piece near it, and rams pieces of a tenth and three times its mass |
| `machinery.ts` | The machinery on the hangar tunnels' walls, laid out from the battleship's seed: frames, catwalks, pipe banks, cable bundles and trays, ducts, tanks, pumps, heat exchangers, cabinets and their small lights, in layers inside the wall; each tunnel with its emphasis (coolant, power, air); each wall drawn flat into a picture (`Elevation`) that stands in for it from outside the hangar deck; `machinery.test.ts` keeps it inside the walls, alike from a seed and each tunnel's under its triangle budget, its pictures drawn and the full machinery shown only from close by, one tunnel's at a time |
| `torpedoes.ts` | Torpedoes: launch, the flight (top speed, nose first, a seeker that reads its target late, less agile than a ship), the fuse at the hull and at whatever solid it meets (rock, a ring, wreckage, a core, a probe, another's torpedo, a comet's head), burnout, shot down by lasers, the missile as built (a lathed body with ogive nose, the seeker's eye, strakes and fins, the nozzle's glowing throat); one on a hook's rope (a bomb on a string: its motor against the rope, its fuse live for every ship, the kill its holder's) |
| `comet.ts` | Comets crossing the arena: schedule (none in the flight school) and route (bent and swallowed by a well), the shower's pieces, the head breaking rock and breaking up on the first hull it meets, which takes its momentum, the head breaking up on the battleship and the shower bursting on its hull and on whatever else is solid, the comet as the scene shows it (head: the nucleus of world/nucleus, tumbling, near or far mesh by distance and the picture's quality; jets from its sunlit vents bending back into the coma; coma, dimmed up close; the two harmless tails streaming away from the sun, the shower glinting orange, dust streaking past inside a tail) and how deep a point lies in a tail |
| `items.ts` | Sponsor probes and what they carry (the nova core's reach), and how they look (a worn hull, solar wings under glass, neon that dims up close); a probe on a hook's cable, and its flight back to its slot once the cable is gone; a probe bouncing off whatever solid it meets (rock, the battleship, a ring, wreckage, a core) |
| `hook.ts` | The hook: harpoon (it bites rock, ships, probes, torpedoes and a core of Capture the Flag), cable, winch (one force on both ends, so a probe comes in fast and barely moves its ship), swing; the cable as a rope between two bodies (see world/bodies), also a core's tow cable, and the tug of war on it; the cables' lines; which of two hooks that bit one probe or torpedo at once keeps it (the lower id, alike on every browser); how close the winch reels a ship to a rock (`clearOf`, `floorOf`); the pull of a cable that lies on the field's pieces along each end's own stretch (see `cable.ts`) |
| `cable.ts` | A cable lying on the field's pieces (the hook's, a core's tow cable): wrap points where a stretch would cut into rock, ice, junk or a hull section, kept in the piece's frame, let go when the cable lifts off; where it snaps instead (the well, the battleship's hull, a piece sweeping through it, too many wraps); the path each end pulls along |
| `racingline.ts` | The ideal line through gates or rings, bowing around rock as an elastic band that bends no tighter than the autopilot turns at the speed limit, with a quarter to spare; where a racer flies for (back on a wide loop to a gate it missed); how tight a line bends |
| `laps.ts` | Lap clock, splits, the best lap and its ghost |
| `hangar.ts` | Solar Coins, the wallet's cap, upgrades and their prices; what other browsers are told of a ship's upgrades (its looks and radiators); the hangar as the browser keeps it, and what in it only an edit puts there |
| `school.ts` | The flight school: five courses, their drills and markers (the orbit drill flown at the Event Horizon, the others in the default arena; ring courses tilted clear of rock; debris that bursts on rock); the completed courses as the browser keeps them |
| `coach.ts` | Nicole's briefings and tips in real rounds, the mouse hint of a Host's first rounds among them |
| `friendly.ts` | Friendly fire: what a kill changes the killer's score by, a kill of the own side taking as much as one of the other side gives |
| `streaks.ts` | Kill streaks: every ship's run of kills close after each other and the bonus its kills earn, a share of a kill's points |
| `moments.ts` | The own pilot's big moments (streaks, revenge, a knockout, …): banner, crowd and announcer |
| `sponsors.ts`, `satire-ads.ts` | The sponsor break before a respawn, how long a Host's and a bot's next clone takes, and the invented sponsors; `sponsors.test.ts` holds a bot to a Host's soonest wait |

Every rule here is also written down in the repository's README with its numbers; a change to one changes the other in the same commit. The `*.test.ts` files test the deterministic parts (`npm test`); `courses.test.ts` flies every race course, and every course of the flight school in the default arena, with a bot of middling skill, the orbit drill at the Event Horizon, and the dash into cover from the cover drill's torpedo; `playability.test.ts`, `hostrace.test.ts` and `hostrace2.test.ts` fly them with a simulated Host at the mouse (see `src/test/human.ts`): what it cannot fly, no Host will; `torpedoes.test.ts` flies torpedoes against ships flying straight, breaking, jinking and dodging; `school.test.ts` judges a drill done before failed, so the last torpedo's hit and a kill with the shot that overheats count.
