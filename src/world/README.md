# src/world

The arena a round is fought in. Everything that every browser must build alike (the field's layout, the rock shapes, fragments) comes from seeded randomness, so peers see the same world.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `arenas.ts` | The seven arenas: field layout, well, air, sun, light and color mood (`MOODS`), storms, the fixed references the HUD marks; which one a tournament round uses; the default arena (the Rings of Saturn) |
| `air.ts` | Jupiter's air: its density by the altitude over the cloud deck, the haze summed along a line and how far the eye, the laser, the radar and a lock reach through it (the deck a veil on top), the drag on a ship over a step, the heat in its hull and the safe speed by depth |
| `reference.ts` | The reference plane through the field's center: height, climb, the bank's sky pointer, closure rate, an arena's fixed references |
| `fracture.ts` | How rock and ice break: every shape's fixed tree of pieces (3 at the first break, 2 or 3 of each at the second), cut from its own meshes around seeded points along rough fracture faces (the same surface on both sides of a cut, so the pieces fit together), each a closed mesh of the parent's outer surface and the fresh faces; their meshes near and far (in the shape's frame, so they read its relief map), their measured surfaces, centroids and shares of the shape's volume, cut by a worker ahead of time and kept (tested in `fracture.test.ts`) |
| `fracture.worker.ts` | Cuts the pieces of a shape's piece in a background worker (see fracture.ts, `prefetch`) and hands their arrays back |
| `surface.ts` | A rock's (or a piece's) measured surface, as collisions see it; measured from a mesh, or from points a worker measured (`measureRadii`) |
| `asteroids.ts` | The field: rocks, ice, debris or hull (shoved by a comet), a disk with pieces in the open space above and below it, orbits, gravity, collisions, raycasts, line of sight, whether something solid lies between two points (a cable cut); a broken rock or chunk of ice split into the pieces of its shape (see fracture.ts), each starting where it lay, the junk into smaller copies of whole shapes; how close up a rock or an ice piece keeps its near mesh before the far one, then a billboard, takes over (the picture's quality) |
| `rocks.ts` | Generates the 16 asteroid shapes with integer math, identical on every browser: a full-detail shape for collisions (its vertices only, never drawn), light near and far meshes for drawing, and a billboard (see view/impostor) for the belt and for rocks and ice far from the field. The meshes' fine relief and color are a baked relief map per shape, not triangles: a 6 × 128² cube map of the surface's object-space normal and shade with dense small craters (raised rims, fresh bright floors), grooves along the long axis, cracks, boulders and grain on top of the shape's own lumps and craters; up close a shared, tiling detail map (smallest craters, pebbles) laid on from three sides, fading out by 450 m. The cube bake, the patch of a material with a relief map and the billboards' views (where each cell meets the shape and how far inside its silhouette, marched through the shape's bump; the billboard paints the relief map there) are shared with ice, the bake and the patch with the comet's nucleus too (tested in `rocks.test.ts`) |
| `ice.ts` | The Rings of Saturn's ice: the rock shapes and their collisions, with a relief map of their own (rounded frost-crusted masses, soft impacts, sublimation pits with dusty floors, flat glassy fracture planes, crack networks, dust specks and bands; its alpha the kind of ice), a detail tile of their own (frost grain, small fractures, pits), a material that answers like ice (cool white, glossy fractures, blue cracks, sunlight through thin parts, frost grains glittering as the chunk turns), its broken pieces' fresh faces clear glassy ice that glints, and billboards in its colors (tested in `ice.test.ts`) |
| `nucleus.ts` | A comet's nucleus, generated with integer math like the rocks: an elongated body of two lobes joined by a neck, one lobe larger and the two at an angle, as one closed mesh (the shared sphere's vertices moved out along their directions, so it never folds over itself), near and far; its relief map baked by the rocks' cube bake (a crust of dark dust reflecting about 4 %, smooth dust plains in the neck and in basins, rough ground pitted with small hollows and broken into terraces ending in cliffs, six sinkholes with flat floors and steep walls, bright ice on cliffs, sinkhole walls and ragged patches; its alpha the brightness) and a material patched with it, the rocks' detail tile up close; the sinkholes as the vents the comet's jets stream from (see game/comet). Looks only: the head strikes as a sphere (tested in `nucleus.test.ts`) |
| `relief.worker.ts` | Bakes the relief maps of rock and ice shapes and of the comet's nucleus in a background worker (see rocks.ts, `bakeReliefMaps`): the game starts a few of them while it loads |
| `bodies.ts` | Bodies and their masses (ship, core, probe, torpedo, wreckage, the field's pieces by volume and density, the battleship; rings immovable); the rope that only pulls (between two browsers as a stiff spring, each end pulled where it is run) and the bump, both split by mass |
| `shapes.ts` | The Orbital Freedom Network's junk (satellites in gold or silver foil with their antennas, radiator and single wing, upper stages with their domes, adapter ring and engine, fairing halves lined inside, stacks of satellites never released) and the world ship's hull sections (three of them with its paint; their collider boxes as `HULL_BOXES`, for tests too), their plating (see view/surfaces) taken by every part at a place and mirroring of its own |
| `worldship.ts` | What the world ship PAAA 01's hull carries besides its plating: its paint as decals on the flanks (see view/lettering; up close their outline is redrawn sharp where a texel spans many pixels), its windows (steady, and two failing circuits that stutter in bursts), its red beacons blinking slowly, its shattered biodomes with their frozen gardens, and the frost on its shadow side and in the plating's recesses, patched into the hull's materials while the Derelict is in play, with the plating's tone varying in patches about a hundred meters across |
| `derelict.ts` | The Derelict's break: the mist of ice crystals escaping from both torn ends (one draw call of point sprites, fewer on a cheaper picture) and how thick it is anywhere (for the patter on a hull flying through), and the ship's dead drifting frozen around it (two instanced bodies), scenery only |
| `gates.ts` | The rings of the tournament as structures (coil housings in paints of their own with worn hazard paint, windings of copper wire glowing, struts, lamps under guards, a station module with a sponsor's plate), all solid but the lights, shared by race gates, school rings and Capture the Flag bases |
| `powercore.ts` | The sponsor core of Capture the Flag as a structure: a reactor canister in dark armor with glowing windings of copper wire, sign plates and a cage for the tow cable; fades to its outline |
| `boxes.ts` | A sphere or a ray against a box (wreck and battleship hulls) |
| `wrecks.ts` | The pieces a destroyed ship breaks into: torn, curved plates with jagged outlines built in code, the fighter's worn paint outside and its innards (frames, stringers, primer) inside, their torn edges glowing as they cool (a vertex attribute of heat in the shader); their flight, collisions and snapshot for a browser that joins |
| `arena.ts` | The arena's edge and its grid |
| `belt.ts` | The asteroid belt beyond the arena, scenery only; close to its inner edge real low-poly rocks, the rest of the ring billboards (see view/impostor); a cheaper picture draws a share of it |
| `mining.ts` | The Rockborn's mining in the Belt: rigs of worn, riveted steel (rust, grime, dents, stencils; hazard stripes on their rims and booms; chamfered beams; dust settled towards the rock; a cab, lit windows and screens, lamps under hoods and in housings) with work lights, cutting lasers with sparks and a glowing cut, dust from drill holes, rocks cut half away with smooth faces and lit tunnels, beacon buoys, neon graffiti, and dust clouds between the rocks glowing against the sun; placed from the field's seed on the rocks' measured surfaces, turning with its rocks through a uniform array of their matrices, seven draw calls in all, thinner by the picture's quality; how thick the drill dust is where the own ship flies, for the patter on its hull |
| `landmarks.ts` | Each arena's scenery: planets, the sun (made by `corona.ts`), the black hole (its disk as smooth glowing gas filling a thin slab, summed along each ray, white-hot at the inner edge to orange and dark at the outer, in soft clumps turning with the gas, its far side swollen so the lens bends it into a broad halo, much brighter and whiter where it streams towards the pilot; on a cheaper picture its smaller clumps and finer sums left out; the hole itself no mesh: view/lensing draws its shadow, bends the disk around it and draws the disk's near side across it with the same shader code, `diskGas`). The Derelict's break (made by `derelict.ts`). Saturn and Jupiter from real maps (`public/assets/planets/`), sharp or light by the picture's quality (see view/quality), Jupiter's cap (its cloud deck, made by `jupiter.ts`) at a cheaper segment count on a cheaper picture; Saturn's ring with the planet's shadow on it and its own on the planet, worked out in the shaders, its clumps and wakes along the orbit too; the ring's sheet (the faces of a slab beyond the layer's chunks, so it never cuts through the ice, each showing the part of the slab beyond it; a disk from afar, more see-through the nearer the pilot's height comes to the layer, a faint veil in it, always closing into a band towards the horizon, never a hole around the pilot), the specks of ice around the pilot that show its layer, and the grains of the layer close by (streaking past a ship flying through, fewer on a cheaper picture), lit by the sun's shadow map, so everything in the field shadows them, and glinting |
| `jupiter.ts` | Jupiter besides its map: the cloud deck as a see-through sheet seen from both sides, its tops made turbulent in code over the map (eddies, sharp-edged billows and their grain, threads along the shear, vortices wound into spirals, white ovals, the great red storm turning as one spiral, pop-up clouds with soft shadows, lit from the side by the low sun; the colors pushed a little towards cool whites and deep browns), all from one tiling noise texture made in code (read back as the GPU reads it in `jupiter.test.ts`), fading out where finer than a few pixels, two thinner sheets under it and the haze's floor below them (under the deck a sphere of haze around the eye), wisps of cloud streaming past under the deck, the wind that carries them and the haze's clouds, lightning, the auroras over the south pole (a thin band on the planet's rim as high as their shell of the upper air rises over it: one smooth, continuous layer of light starting at the clouds' edge, pale and brightest at its foot, violet above and fading out upwards, brighter and taller only in broad stretches drifting slowly, breathing gently over seconds, held still with reduced motion; one draw call, its strip laid in the vertex shader), Io and Europa; the haze's light from the map's colors around the arena; fewer sheets, wisps, layers of the auroras and layers of the tops on a cheaper picture |
| `junkyard.ts` | The Satellite Junkyard's scenery: Earth at its real size 5,000 km below, a whole ball 68° across, the terminator running through it, its maps drawn as seen from far above the point beneath the arena: the day side towards the sun from its day map (`earth-day-*.jpg`), lit through the air as on Earth (the low sun reddening, the clouds catching its last light, the twilight fading), the night away from it from its night map (`public/assets/planets/earth-*.jpg`), its lights grown into the 26th century and coming on as the sky darkens, the air along its horizon, satellites burning up in it, drawn behind everything; the junk beyond the field on its orbits about Earth as one draw call of point sprites, in the colors of the field's junk; the network's flickering billboard |
| `corona.ts` | The Corona's sun: its face boiling with cells, darkening at the limb, its sunspots (umbra, penumbra of filaments, faculae) drifting across as it turns on the field time; the chromosphere's red rim and the inner corona's streamers on a quad around it; the prominences as one mesh of ribbons along magnetic loops over the limb, rising and fading over minutes with coronal rain sliding down them; the sky's scattered light around it (never black) and the veil its glare lays over the picture, two quads over the whole picture by each pixel's angle from the sun; fewer prominences and no finest grain on a cheaper picture, no heat shimmer |
| `flare.ts` | The solar flare's plasma on screen: a sphere of roiling fire with filaments |
| `space.ts` | Stars, the arena's nebulae and mood (`setMood`: space's color, the sky's light from above and below or, in the Corona, from the sun's side and the far side, how bright the stars, the dust's tint), the sun's light and shadows (heated by a solar flare, dimmed by a planet's haze over the camera; their map's size or none at all, as the picture's quality says), the sky's light (taking the haze's colors under a cloud deck), dust and its streaks at speed (`dustStreaks`, also the dust of a comet's tail); the `shelter`, a box the dust and the Rings' specks and grains stay out of (the battleship's hangar deck) |
| `nebula.ts` | What an arena's light and color mood is made of (`Mood`, `Nebula`), and the soft cloud shapes its nebulae are drawn from (`cloudTexture`, made once from integer hashes) |

`junkyard.test.ts` checks that the junkyard lies in sunlight above Earth's terminator, that Earth is a whole ball within the camera's view looking straight down, with day and full night each in a good part of it, that Earth's maps cover every part of the cap that is built and the day map every part the sun or the twilight reaches, and that its junk stays beyond the reach of any ship; `derelict.test.ts` that the Derelict's mist is thick only on its jets out of the torn ends, that its dead drift and turn slowly and stay by the break, and that its paint uses only letters the stencil has; `mining.test.ts` that the Belt's mining stands alike on every browser, on whole rocks only, its cut faces never more than their tolerance above the measured surface, its drill holes in the surface and its dust clouds clear of the rocks; `asteroids.test.ts`, `arenas.test.ts` and `wrecks.test.ts` check layouts, snapshots and collisions (and that no field piece switches to its billboard within the arena across; that a wreck's piece is drawn about as far as its collisions reach, its skin outside and its innards inside, every triangle wound the way it faces; that a broken rock's pieces start where they lay, fill the rock and nothing more, weigh what it did and fly apart, break once more, and come out alike on every browser and for a newcomer), `fracture.test.ts` that every shape breaks into 3 pieces and each of those into 2 or 3, none a sliver, every piece a closed mesh, their volumes adding up to their parent's, their outer surface the rock's own and their fracture faces rough, cut the same every time, `bodies.test.ts` the rope and the bump, `air.test.ts` Jupiter's air (density, the eye's reach by depth, the haze along a line against a ray's reach, the deck hiding a ship under it, drag, the speed the drive holds against it, heat and the safe speed, the hull burnt only by what full radiators cannot shed), `reference.test.ts` the reference plane in every arena, height, climb, the sky pointer, the closure rate and each arena's fixed references, `mood.test.ts` that every arena's mood is its own, its nebulae few, and that its grade leaves the HUD's azure and orange their hue, `rocks.test.ts` that a rock's relief map, read as WebGL picks a cube map's texel for a direction, holds the surface normal and the shade of the place that texel stands for (and read mirrored would not), that the detail map repeats without a seam and keeps a rock's mean tone and tilt, that a billboard's views, read as the GPU reads the array texture, keep the drawn rock where its ray meets it (and read mirrored would not), hold the place there its relief map is read at, point just off the edge where the rock lies nearest, and paint the far mesh's very map in its colors, and that the shapes the collisions measure are the drawn rocks vertex for vertex (`npm test`).

`landmarks.test.ts` checks how much of Saturn's ring its sheet shows by the camera's height: the whole ring from afar on the face across the slab and nothing on the near face, a faint veil in the layer that never vanishes, smoothly and alike on either side of the plane.

`shapes.test.ts` reads the texture coordinates of the junk's livery back, triangle by triangle: on every stage and fairing, outside and lining, the texture runs the way a page does as seen from the face's own side, so no stencil shows mirrored.

`jupiter.test.ts` reads the noise of Jupiter's cloud tops back as the GPU reads it: it repeats without a seam in every channel, its noise channels center on 0.5 so they fade to their mean where mipmapped, and about a third of its cells carry a pop-up cloud, the same every time.
