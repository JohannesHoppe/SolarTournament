      Controls

      Mouse / Arrows
      Turn: the ship turns towards the pointer
      W
      Main drive
      S
      Brake: flip and burn to a stop
      A / D
      Thrusters: slide sideways
      Shift
      Fine aim
      Space / Click
      Fire laser
      X (hold)
      Flip: nose against the flight; with W it brakes
      T
      Autopilot (flies where it points) / free drift
      E
      Overdrive: one shove forward, last resort, once a minute
      R / F
      Extras: thrusters up and down
      A D R F twice
      Dodge: a hard kick that way
      G
      Torpedo: homes on the ship ahead, two per clone
      V / Right click (hold)
      Hook: bite a rock or a ship, reel in, let go to fly off
      Gamepad
      Sticks turn and push, RT drive, B brake, LT/A fire, Y flip, R3 autopilot, RB overdrive, LB dodge, D-pad up torpedo, D-pad down hook
      C
      Cockpit / chase camera
      H
      Cinema mode: no HUD, a softer camera, for recordings
      M
      Radio on / off
      Esc / P
      Pause; Q there quits the round
      Tab (hold)
      Standings: kills, deaths, score, in a race the lap
